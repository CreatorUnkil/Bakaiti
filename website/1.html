Account management

How do I reset my password?

Please note that we cannot reset passwords or reactivate Bakaiti accounts. If you have an email address attached to your account, you can reset your password here.

Do I need an email address to register?

Using an email address when creating a Bakaiti account is optional; it will make it easier for others to discover you and can help you to reset your password if needed. It's ultimately up to the administrator of the server to decide whether or not you need to use an email address. For accounts on the Bakaiti homeserver, supplying an email address during registration is optional, but we reserve the right to occasionally require it for abuse mitigation as referred to in the privacy policy.

What is the username used for?

We will use your username to create a Matrix ID allowing to disambiguate you from others with the same display name. It will allow us to keep your email confidential. 🙂

Can I register with a phone number?

Not anymore. Sorry!

Chat

How can I mention someone?

Just type their name and they will be notified accordingly. You can autocomplete by pressing the tab button, you don’t need any prefix for the mention to work!

With what type of mentions will I get notified?

By default you’ll be notified if your display name and your user name are mentioned. You can add other names, nicknames or keywords on which you want to be notified from your settings, in the Notifications section.

How do I send a file?

Simple, just drag the file into Bakaiti and it will automatically upload. Alternatively you can click on the paperclip icon in the text input field and browse your filesystem.

Can I upload a file from a mobile device?

Yes you can! On a mobile device simply press the arrow pointing upwards (iOS) or the + symbol (Android) while in a chat and select your file.

How can I invite a contact to use Bakaiti?

You can invite someone to a room by using the "Invite to this room" button in the right hand side "Room members list" using their Matrix ID (if they have one) or by email. Those without a Matrix ID will be able to preview the room if the room allows for that.

Is there a way to know if a message has been read?

Yes! Bakaiti shows who has read a message by displaying their avatar to the right of the message. Hovering over these avatars (or clicking on them on mobile) will give you the user’s info and reading time.

How can I search for a file or message?

To search a room from Desktop, click on the magnifying glass located near the top of the screen. You can then type in a keyword or filename that you are searching for with the ability to select whether you want to search within the specific chat room or across all conversations. For encrypted rooms, search only works on Desktop (not Bakaiti web and mobile) and you need to have "Message search" enabled in the Security & Privacy settings.

Settings

How do I change my account settings?

Click on the drop down menu under your name in the top left corner of the web or desktop app and select Settings. From here you can change all of your account and general Bakaiti preferences.

How do I change my notification settings?

Bakaiti allows you to customise your notifications at two levels: across the app and per room. You can configure how you will be notified for given events by default in the Notifications section of your Settings.

  • Turned off: you won’t be notified.
  • Turned on: message popup, but no sound.
  • “Noisy”: visual highlight, sound and/or vibration.

Per room: Mute, Mentions only, All messages, All messages (noisy).

How do I set up email notifications?

You can set Bakaiti up to email you when you have missed some activity. Enable “Email notifications” in the Notification section of your Settings.

How can I change my display name?

You can change your display name in General section of your Settings.

How do I reset my password?

The Bakaiti team won't be able to reset passwords or reactivate accounts. If you have forgotten your password, visit app.bakaiti.chat and click “Set a new password”. You will need to input the email address registered to your account. An email will be sent to confirm your request. If you haven't set an email address, please contact your homeserver administrator.

Why would I need to associate an email address to my account?

We strongly recommend that you set an email address in order to be able to reclaim your account if you’ve lost your password. It is also useful for people to find you. If you haven’t done so at registration, you can add as many email addresses as you like, at any point, in the General section of your Settings.

Rooms

How can I change the settings for a specific chat or room?

You can change the settings for any one-to-one chat or group room by clicking on the cog icon next to the room name.

Can I restrict the access of a room to a given set of people?

You can restrict the access of a room to people who have been invited by selecting the “Private (invite only)” option in the "Security & Privacy" settings of the room. People knowing the link of the room won’t be able to access it if an invite hasn’t been explicitly sent.

Can I limit the access of a room to people knowing its existence?

You can restrict the access of a room to people with whom you shared a link to the room by selecting the “anyone who knows the room link” option in the room settings. Selecting this option will not make the room publicly visible.

Can I make a room publicly discoverable?

You can get your room listed into your server’s directory by checking the button labeled "Publish this room to the public in example.com's room directory?". By checking this box, it will be discoverable to anyone searching for a room on the server.

Can new members read the history of the room?

Yes, if the room history visibility is set to “Members (full history)” in the room’s Privacy & Security settings. In encrypted rooms, the feature is under development. When you change the history visibility, it won’t affect messages already sent.

Why would I want to make history visible to anyone?

Making history visible to anyone means that people can see what is being said in a room before joining it. This gives users the opportunity to “peek” into a room. This option is only offered when the room is accessible to anyone and not end-to-end encrypted.

What is a room address?

Setting an address for a room allows you to give it a simple reference, making it easier to share a link. Addresses are linked to the server you are registered on.

What is a favourite room / low priority / historical?

Favourite – pin important rooms at the top. Low priority – move less important rooms to the bottom. Historical – lists rooms you’ve left, showing past messages.

As a room admin, can I decide what members can do?

In the roles and permissions section of the room’s settings, you’ll be able to configure the privilege levels required to perform various actions: send message, ban/kick, redact, invite, etc.

Privacy & Encryption

How is my personal data being used?

Here is Bakaiti’s privacy policy. If you have additional questions, contact our DPO at dpo@bakaiti.chat.

How do I report on content in Bakaiti?

Report inappropriate content by hovering on the message, clicking the more options button (three dots) and "Report content".

How do I submit an abuse report or DMCA takedown?

For abuse on the Bakaiti homeserver, contact abuse@bakaiti.chat. For DMCA, please read our Copyright Policy and send notices to dmca@bakaiti.chat.

What is end-to-end encryption?

End-to-end encryption means your messages and files are encrypted before they leave your device, and stay encrypted until they reach the other participants' devices. Only the participants can read them.

What is key storage / recovery key?

Key storage is necessary to decrypt your chat history on new devices. Recovery key is a 48-character key generated to restore your chat backup if you lose access to all devices. Store it safely!

What does “key storage is out of sync” mean?

When one or more keys are missing, you are notified and asked to enter your recovery key to retrieve missing identity keys. If key storage does not have all keys, you will need to reset your digital identity.

What is identity pinning / user verification?

Identity pinning saves a contact’s identity automatically. User verification (emoji or QR code) adds extra protection against man-in-the-middle attacks.

Why do I see a digital identity reset alert?

You are notified whenever a contact's digital identity has been reset. Confirm with your contact whether they intentionally reset it. If previously verified, we strongly recommend to re-verify.

Threads Beta & Reporting

Why does my homeserver need to support threads?

In order to reliably update the list of threads, your homeserver needs to support threads via MSC3440. Synapse 1.55 and above supports it. Ask your administrator to upgrade.

How does the room list unread badge work with threads?

The badge shows the number of messages between your last read receipt and the last event. Some “ghost count” may appear, but an unread dot will be shown in the thread list until you’ve opened the thread.

How do I submit a bug report or request a feature?

Bug report: Mobile → Settings → Report a problem. Desktop/web → Settings → Feedback → Start a new one.
Feature request: Check existing issues on GitHub (Bakaiti Web/Desktop, Bakaiti X iOS, Bakaiti X Android).
